Privacy policy · Updated 13 October 2026

Who operates this calendar

Tech Calendar at calendar.korvexa.dev is a product of Korvexa Dev, based in Malawi. For privacy enquiries, email hello@korvexa.com.

Saved events and browser storage

Bookmarks are stored on your device using browser local storage under korvexa-saved. They remain until you unsave them or clear that storage. We do not create an account or upload your saved list as a user profile.

If you export or subscribe to your saved selection, its event identifiers are included in the calendar-feed request. Search terms and filters can also appear in page URLs, shared links and requests to our server.

Website requests

Serving a page or calendar file sends technical information to our hosting infrastructure, including your IP address, requested URL, browser headers and request time. Hosting providers may process this information and keep operational logs to deliver the service, diagnose errors and prevent abuse.

We use technical request information to operate and secure the calendar. Log retention depends on the hosting configuration. Information sent by email is used to respond to your enquiry and retained as needed to handle it and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Advertising and cookies

This calendar uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. The Google AdSense script loads on every page and may place cookies and use local identifiers, your IP address, and device or activity information to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information across other websites and services. You can review and manage Google’s ad personalisation settings at adssettings.google.com ↗.

Learn how Google uses information from partner sites ↗ and view Google’s privacy policy ↗.

The public Google publisher identifier is published at ads.txt as required by the IAB Tech Lab publisher declaration standard.

Other websites and calendar apps

Event websites, Google Calendar and other external services follow their own privacy policies. Adding an event to Google Calendar sends the selected event’s public title, dates, description and location to Google. Downloaded calendar files contain event information for the calendar app you choose.

Your choices

You can clear bookmarks here or through your browser settings. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, or complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Contact us to discuss a request.

Browser bookmarks are held on your device; clearing them is the direct way to remove that saved list. You can manage Google’s ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com ↗.

Policy updates

We will revise this page when the calendar’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
